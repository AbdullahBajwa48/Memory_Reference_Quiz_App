Instruction set

16-bit word · first hex digit selects op and mode, last three digits are the address field.

HexOpEffect
0 · 8ANDAC ← AC ∧ M
1 · 9ADDAC ← AC + M, E ← carry
2 · ALDAAC ← M
3 · BSTAM ← AC
4 · CBUNPC ← EA
5 · DBSAM ← PC, PC ← EA + 1
6 · EISZM ← M + 1, skip if 0
  • 0–6 direct: EA = address field
  • 8–E indirect: EA = low 12 bits of M[address field]
  • M means M[EA] · PC already points at the next instruction · skip: PC ← PC + 1
